## Methodology Notes

- Confidence = weighted avg of LB (×2), CM (×1.5), GC (×1.5) minus positional penalty (up to −30) and n-gram penalty (up to −25).
- Values with `?` (e.g., `pa2?`, `de?`) treated as uncertain, not confident LB evidence.
- REVISE only when ≥2 independent sources agree on a new value different from conventional.
- UNCERTAIN when sources conflict or only one source has a confident value.
