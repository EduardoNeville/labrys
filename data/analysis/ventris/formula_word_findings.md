## 8.1 di-ki-* word family (SOLID structure)

The root di-ki- (AB 07 AB 67) appears in site-specific forms:

| Form | Site | Inscriptions | Scholarship |
|------|------|--------------|-------------|
| di-ki-te-te | Palaikastro | PKZa8, PKZa11, PKZa12, PKZa15 | ✅ confirms published JA-DI-KI-TE-TE-DU-PU |
| di-ki-tu-ja | Iouktas | IOZa2 | the IOZa2 form |
| di-ki-se, di-ki-qa-qa | Haghia Triada | HT87, HT117b, HT118 | — |
| di-ki-o | Zakros | ZA27 | — |

**Palaikastro contexts:**
- PKZa8: `e-ja-di-ki-te-te-pu-re-tu-me`
- PKZa11: `e-a-di-ki-te-te-re-?-te-ri`
- PKZa15: `ja-di-ki-te-te-du-pu-re`

**Interpretation (HYPOTHESIS):** di-ki- is a deity/epithet root (Dikte-related),
with site-specific suffixation (-te-te at Palaikastro, -tu at Iouktas). This
matches the scholarship's "plausibly connected with Mount Dikte".

## 8.2 ja- prefix (SOLID enrichment, speculative meaning)

- AB 57 (ja) is 11.3% of libation signs vs 3.3% of whole corpus = **3.4× enriched**
- ja- words: ja-sa-sa (12×), ja-di-ki (3×), others singleton
- ja-sa-sa-ra-me alternates with a-sa-sa-ra-me (scholarship) — ja-/a- optional

**Interpretation (HYPOTHESIS, explicitly NOT established):** ja- could be a
vocative/particle ("O..."). Scholarship: NO settled meaning. Linear A is
undeciphered; case endings cannot be reliably identified.

## Honest status

**SOLID (structural, verified on corrected corpus):**
1. The libation formula has fixed recurring words (ja-sa-sa-ra-me,
   u-na-ka-na-si, si-ru-te) in fixed order
2. di-ki-te-te at Palaikastro matches the published reading
3. ja- is enriched 3.4× in libation contexts

**HYPOTHESIS (not established):**
1. di-ki- = Dikte deity (scholarship says "plausibly connected")
2. ja- = vocative (scholarship: no settled meaning)

**The structural facts are the asset.** The phonetic values of the formula
words are LB-transfer (the project's grid basis), and the formula provides
fixed-position constraints — which is what Priority 11 tests.
