## Structural summary

1. **Opening** `a-?-i-wa-ja` (A-TA-I-*301-WA-JA) at Iouktas (IOZa2/3/7/8),
   Syme (SYZa1/2/3/4/8), Kophinas (KOZa1), Palaikastro (PKZa11/12 variant)
2. **ja-sa-sa-ra-me** — the most widespread formula word (Iouktas,
   Palaikastro, Psykhro, Platanos)
3. **u-na-ka-na-si** — follows ja-sa-sa-ra-me (Iouktas, Palaikastro) or
   stands alone (Syme, Kophinas)
4. **si-ru-te** — the closing element (Iouktas, Syme, Kophinas)
5. **di-ki-*** — site-specific deity-root (Palaikastro: -te-te; Iouktas: -tu-ja)
6. **i-da** appears in the formula context (SYZa1: i-da-?-ja; ZA21b:
   i-da-ki-sa-ri-me; KOZa1: i-da-a) — the Ida mountain reference

## Caveats

- `?` = sign without a value in the grid (LB-transfer gaps)
- The catalog is structural — phonetic values are LB-transfer
- Arkalochori (ARKH) texts have NO formula words (different corpus)
