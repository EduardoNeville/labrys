# Phase 11, Avenue 6 — Diachronic Prior: Findings and Use

## What we established

### 1. Persistence → confirmation (SOLID, verified)
Signs attested in BOTH MM (~1700 BCE) and LM (~1450 BCE) periods are 2× more
likely to be CONFIRMED (67% vs 33%, Fisher p=0.0003). Oracle LOO: 1.21× lift
over majority. NOT a frequency artifact (shared-confirmed median LM freq 2.5
vs 3.0).

### 2. Persistence → confidence (SOLID, independent of phonetic evidence)
MM-attested mean refined confidence 51.0 vs LM-only 28.4 (1.8× gap, n=45 vs
93). Critically, this holds even when the LB/CM confidence components are
EMPTY (e.g., AB 62, AB 34 have grid confidence but no lb/cm) — the signal is
structural, not a recapitulation of the phonetic evidence.

### 3. MM-attested conflicts are more settled (CONSISTENT, partially circular)
Among 10 LB-vs-CM conflicts: MM-attested (5) mean conf 64.7 vs LM-only (5)
52.5. All refined toward LB. Consistent with the prior, but the confidence
itself derives from LB/CM, so partially circular.

### 4. Persistence ≠ functional stability (CAUTION — undermines naive use)
Positional behavior is NOT conserved across periods: AB 49 goes 67%→7%
initial, AB 77 83%→7%. The inventory persists but *usage* evolves. So the
prior is about *existence*, not *function*.

## What the prior is and isn't

**It is:** a Bayesian prior on phonetic confidence, independent of LB/CM
evidence. For any sign: MM-attested → raised prior (likely real value),
LM-only → lowered prior (likely late/rare/uncertain).

**It is not:** a value-assignment tool. It re-weights which signs to bet on,
it doesn't say what they mean.

## Concrete use in the decipherment

1. **Prioritize MM-attested UNCERTAIN signs.** The 4 MM-attested UNCERTAIN
   signs (AB 16, AB 82, AB 89, AB 90) get the highest prior for having real
   values. AB 16 is the keystone qa/ka conflict — the prior raises its
   stakes. AB 82/89/90 are low-conf signs worth re-examining.
2. **Downgrade LM-only conflict expectations.** AB 60 (the ra/ma keystone) is
   LM-only — under the prior, its chance of being a secure value is LOWER
   than previously assumed. This is a real, falsifiable adjustment: the most
   famous open question in the project is now *less* likely to resolve.
3. **Bayesian combination.** The prior multiplies into any future scorer
   (oracle-tested): conf_adjusted = conf_base × (2.0 if MM else 0.5). The
   oracle can validate whether this improves recovery.

## Honest limits

- The prior is weak (1.2–1.8×), not decisive.
- Positional instability warns against over-interpreting "persistence".
- The 43-sign shared set is small; the MM subset (172 signs) is tiny.
- It cannot resolve a single value by itself.

## Recommendation

The diachronic prior is a genuine, verified, independent signal — the only one
in Phase 10–11 that survived verification as a POSITIVE. Its best use is as a
**re-weighting layer on top of any future evidence**, and as a **prioritization
tool** for which signs to investigate. It should be folded into the synthesis
as a verified prior, not oversold as a decipherment step.
