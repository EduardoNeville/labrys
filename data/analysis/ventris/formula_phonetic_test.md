# Formula Phonetic Test — Results (Priority 11)

**Date:** 2026-08-13
**Status:** CONCLUDED — formula is structurally real, phonetically inert

## The test

Can the libation formula recover phonetic values that the whole-corpus scorer
couldn't? Two tests:

1. **Position → vowel class**: does a sign's position in a formula word
   predict its vowel?
2. **Recurrence**: does the formula's repetition constrain sign identity?

## Results

### Test 1: position → vowel (TAUTOLOGICAL, no signal)

| Word | Vowel pattern |
|------|--------------|
| ja-sa-sa-ra-me | a-a-a-a-e (9×, always) |
| u-na-ka-na-si | u-a-a-a-i (6×, always) |
| si-ru-te | i-u-e (7×, always) |

The vowels are fixed because the SIGNS are fixed. A fixed sign string has
fixed phonetics by definition — position-in-word tells you nothing new.
**No phonetic signal.**

### Test 2: recurrence (real, but structural not phonetic)

- ja-sa-sa-ra-me: 9 occurrences
- u-na-ka-na-si: 6
- si-ru-te: 7
- di-ki-te-te: 3 (Palaikastro)
- di-ki-tu-ja: 1 (Iouktas)

Recurrence confirms the CORPUS is correct (the mis-mapped old corpus broke
the recurrence; the corrected one restores it). But it recovers SIGN
IDENTITY, not phonetics — the values are already known (LB-transfer).

## Verdict

**The formula is structurally real and confirms the correction, but it is
phonetically inert for value recovery.** Its value:
1. Confirms the corrected corpus (recurrence works)
2. Provides fixed word anchors (ja-sa-sa-ra-me, u-na-ka-na-si, si-ru-te)
3. Shows site-specific deity roots (di-ki-te-te Palaikastro, di-ki-tu-ja
   Iouktas, i-da-ki-sa-ri-me Zakros)
4. The -me suffix pattern (ja-sa-sa-ra-me, i-da-ki-sa-ri-me) is real

**But it cannot cascade into NEW phonetic values** — the formula's words
are fixed sign strings whose values come from the (LB-transfer) grid.

## The honest implication

This closes the phonetic-cascade hope for the formula. The formula is a
structural/anchor asset, not a value-recovery tool. The project's conclusion
stands: **without new data or new independent phonetic evidence, the grid
cannot be extended by computation alone.**
