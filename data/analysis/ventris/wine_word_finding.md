# A1 — The Wine Word (qa A 310 i) — Finding

**Date:** 2026-08-13
**Status:** DONE — a structured, site-specific wine-entry template found

## The pattern

Of 10 wine (A 310) contexts in the corrected corpus, **5 at Haghia Triada** share the template:

```
qa  A 310  i
AB 16  (wine logogram)  AB 28
```

| Inscription | Pattern |
|-------------|---------|
| HT8a | `qa A 310 i A 568 si ki ra` |
| HT8b | `qa A 310 i ... ka` |
| HT85b | `qa A 310 i` |
| HT98a | `qa A 310 i` |
| HT122a | `qa A 310 i ... ja ... da` |

The other 5 (HT27a, HT37, KN28a, KNZf31, ZA11a) lack the qa-i structure.

## Interpretation

- **qa (AB 16)** before the wine logogram = a qualifier (measure/grade of wine)
- **i (AB 28)** after = a wine-type element (the start of a wine-type word)
- This is a **Haghia Triada-specific wine-entry template**: qa-wine-i

## Caveats

- AB 16 (qa) is one of the UNCERTAIN signs (qa/ka conflict). Its value here
  is the LB-transfer reading.
- AB 28 (i) is the same sign as in the libation formula (i-*301) and in
  pa-i-to (Phaistos) — a high-frequency, multi-context sign.
- The wine word `i-...` is NOT the libation formula's `i-*301-na-ma` — the
  libation uses A 301, the wine list uses A 310. Distinct contexts.

## Value

A **corrected, positionally-consistent lexical hint**: at Haghia Triada,
wine entries follow `qa A 310 i`. If AB 16 (qa) is ever resolved, this
template constrains the wine-vocabulary reading. For now it's a structural
fact about Haghia Triada accounting practice.
